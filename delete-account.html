Account and Data Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data for Doodle Hop (including cloud scores, coins, and unlocked skins stored in Firebase Authentication and Firestore), you can request it by following these steps:

  1. Send an email to isacell.store@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request - Doodle Hop".
  2. Include the email address or account identifier associated with your Google Sign-In login.

Our support team will process your request and permanently delete your user profile, authentication credentials, and cloud-synced game data from our Firebase database within 7 business days.

Alternative: You can also sign out directly from the app settings or uninstall the application to immediately remove local SQLite game data from your device.
